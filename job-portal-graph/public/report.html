4. Computational Complexity Slopegraph

Analytical, not measured

Theoretical comparison between graph traversal via index-free adjacency and relational multi-table join operations.

Technical Justification

  • Index-Free Adjacency: In the property graph, each node directly points to its neighboring relationship records in memory. Traversing hops requires constant time $O(1)$ per edge pointer, regardless of total graph size.
  • M:N Bridge Tables Replaced: Relational bridge tables (HAS_SKILL, REQUIRES, APPLIES) are replaced by rich property edges carrying proficiency, years, and mandatory attributes directly.
  • Cycle-Safe Traversal vs Recursive CTE: Arbitrary-depth referral queries (-[:REFERS*1..3]->) execute in single-pass traversals without recursive join explosions or self-join locking.

5. Normalisation Summary & Schema Reconciliations

5.1 Normalisation Summary from DA-1

The DA-1 relational architecture decomposed 18 entities into 26 BCNF relations to eliminate insertion, update, and deletion anomalies.

Relational Decomposition (BCNF) Property Graph Representation Rationale
JOB_PRIMARY, JOB_SALARY_INFO, JOB_STATUS_INFO, JOB_BRIDGE Unified (:Job) node BCNF decomposes Job attributes across 4 tables to avoid transitive functional dependencies. The graph encapsulates these directly on the Job node.
CANDIDATE_LOCATION (city ↔ street ↔ pin) (:Candidate)-[:LOCATED_IN]->(:Location) City/state/country hierarchies naturally normalize onto shared Location nodes without synthetic foreign key tables.
Dependent entity Omitted from graph model Family PII with zero relevance to candidate skill matching or job recommendations.

5.2 Technical Reconciliations with DA-1 & DA-2

  • Junction Table Naming: The DA-1 PDF diagram spells the candidate assessment junction Assured_For, whereas the relational schema specification uses ASSESSED_FOR. The graph engine standardizes on ASSESSED_FOR.
  • Proficiency vs Years Comparison Bug: In DA-2, the trigger compared HAS_SKILL.proficiency_level >= REQUIRES.min_year, conflating a 1–5 qualitative score with a temporal duration. The graph implementation reconciles this into two independent criteria: min_proficiency (1–5) and min_years (≥ 0).
  • Mandatory Skill Enforcement: The DA-2 trigger disregarded the is_mandatory column entirely. The graph engine enforces that all mandatory requirements must be satisfied for a job to pass screening.
  • 40% Screening Rule: The DA-2 database trigger TRG_SCREEN_APPLICATION required applicants to meet at least 40% match score. In the graph application, this is implemented as an atomic server-side write transaction that computes Q1 match percentage, validates mandatory skills, and guarantees duplicate deduplication before writing MATCHED_TO and ASSESSED_FOR snapshots.
